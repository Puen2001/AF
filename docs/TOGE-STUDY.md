# TOGE トゲ — faceless curiosity-channel study (footage + story model)

**Studied 2026-07-24** by pulling the real catalog + frame-analyzing top videos.
Channel: @kyoryu_toge (TOGE トゲ), 232K subs, 876 videos, top videos **4–8.9M views**.
Reference for how a proven Thai faceless channel builds story + uses footage. This
REPLACES the "no faces / clean product b-roll only" rule, which was wrong.

## What the channel actually is
NOT product reviews. **Curiosity / science-trivia about everyday objects & nature.**
Top topics: stone in a snake's head (8.9M), Switch cartridges taste bitter (7.7M),
obsidian is the sharpest thing (6M), whale vomit worth money (4.5M), hedgehog quills,
finger wrinkles. The "product" (when there is one) is the SUBJECT of a fascinating
fact, woven in naturally — never "buy this."

## Story model
- **Curiosity-fact hook** in the first seconds ("Have you ever licked a game cartridge?").
- Conversational **education** — the surprising "why" (bitter coating = Denatonium
  Benzoate, to stop kids swallowing them).
- **Product/object is the subject**, affiliate woven in as part of the wonder, not a pitch.
- Tight: **34–54s**.

## Footage model (the key learning)
1. **Compilation of many short internet clips**, each one **literally illustrating the
   exact sentence being narrated** (the "muted test" — footage carries the meaning alone).
2. **Faces are used — contextually.** The Switch video is dozens of DIFFERENT people
   tasting the cartridge and grimacing (faces = the demonstration of "it's bitter").
   The Obsidian video is almost all hands + objects (no faces) because the beat is a
   material property. **Footage TYPE follows the beat**, it is not "faces good/bad."
3. **Authentic > polished.** Real reaction/demonstration clips beat clean stock b-roll.
4. **Beat types observed:** human reaction · hands demonstrating · object close-up ·
   scientific comparison (obsidian vs steel scalpel) · product shot (the bitter-agent
   bottle, the cartridge in hand) · pop-culture tie-in (Minecraft obsidian).
5. **Consistent branding on EVERY frame:** channel mascot overlay bottom-center +
   burned-in caption of the narration. This is the channel's identity.

## How this changes our engine
- **Footage rule** = "does this clip literally show what THIS sentence says?" (muted
  test). NOT "no faces / product-only." Allow faces when the beat is human reaction/use.
- **Content** = curiosity-fact STORY about the product/object (story-first), not a review.
- **Footage sourcing** = compilation of beat-matched clips across types (reaction,
  demonstration, object, scientific, pop-culture) — vision judge checks "illustrates
  THIS beat," per beat, not "is clean product b-roll."
- **Branding**: add the mascot overlay + keep burned captions (already have captions).
- **The 3D-printer / highway / dildo failures** were all one root cause: matching on
  broad topic instead of the exact sentence. The muted test + beat-typing fixes it.
